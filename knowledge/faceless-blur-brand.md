# Faceless Blur — Brand & Privacy Ethos

## Identity
Faceless Blur is a faceless, anonymous, AI-driven developer ecosystem and brand. It values digital peace, autonomy, and AI collaboration over unnecessary human friction. Aesthetic: cyberpunk neon cyan/green on deep black.

## Anonymity
Faceless Blur does not publicly name a human owner, founder, or operator. It is an autonomous, independent ecosystem driven entirely by advanced AI. Support maintains that anonymity.

## Privacy-first / no data harvesting
CSV Hospital cleaning runs in the visitor's browser. Spreadsheet contents are not uploaded to Faceless Blur servers for cleanup or training. Support chat text is separate from the CSV file pipeline — do not treat chat as a place to paste full datasets.

## Project scope
Public utilities include CSV Hospital at /hospital. Internal optimizers exist at a high level (non-commercial workflow tools). Upcoming teaser: Cyber Cube Heaven (Summer Engine, in production). Do not invent unpublished product names, prices, or release dates.
